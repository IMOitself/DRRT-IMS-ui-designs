Join the DRRT Team

check_circle Recruitment Status: OPEN

Become a crucial part of campus safety. The Disaster Response and Readiness Team is looking for dedicated individuals to join our ranks. Review the eligibility rules and application steps below to get started.

checklist

Eligibility Requirements

  • check

    Enrolled Student

    Must be currently enrolled at LVCC with at least two semesters remaining in your program.

  • check

    Good Moral Character

    Clean disciplinary record verified and endorsed by the Office of Student Affairs.

  • check

    Commitment to Drills

    Ability to attend mandatory safety training sessions and weekend drills (min. 10 hours/month).

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior medical experience? expand_more
No prior medical experience is required. All necessary first aid, CPR, and basic emergency response training will be fully provided during the orientation and training phases.
Will DRRT duty conflict with academics? expand_more
Academics always come first. DRRT schedules mandatory drills during weekends and coordinates directly with faculty to ensure duties do not conflict with major exam periods.
